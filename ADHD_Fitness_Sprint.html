Stuck Before Starting?

Your ADHD brain needs a starter spark. You know WHAT to do. But can't START? That's not laziness. That's executive dysfunction. Pick ONE hack below. Then start your Day 1.

☐ OPTION 1: Movement Reset (5 minutes)
  • Take a quick walk, dance, jump, stretch
  • WHY: Movement activates your brain's activation system
  • Then come back + open your Daily Tracker
☐ OPTION 2: Dopamine Jolt (1 minute)
  • Cold water on wrists or face (shocking but fast)
  • Ice in mouth for 10 seconds (acute stimulus)
  • WHY: Cold = automatic dopamine spike in ADHD brains
  • Then start with just 5 minutes of your goal
☐ OPTION 3: Tiny Time Commitment (1 minute)
  • Set a timer: "I'll do this for just 1 minute"
  • Most likely: 1 minute becomes 10+ minutes
  • WHY: No "forever" feeling = brain can start
  • After 1 minute, check the box. Victory.
☐ OPTION 4: Accountability Spark (2 minutes)
  • Text a friend: "Starting my fitness sprint NOW"
  • Or say it out loud to someone
  • WHY: External pressure activates executive function
  • Then start while momentum is on
You don't need willpower. You need a SPARK. You just picked yours above.

If You Missed a Workout

Here are 10 gentle ways to reconnect. Pick ONE. That counts as today.

1. Dance (any song, any length)

Put on your favorite song. Move however feels good. This counts. Movement is movement.

2. Do chores in workout clothes

Wear your gym clothes while cleaning, cooking, or doing anything else. Your body is moving. Your brain registers effort.

3. Take a walk (no speed goal)

Just walk. Slow is perfect. 5 minutes is enough. Fresh air counts.

4. Do 10 squats (or 3, or 5)

Literally just 10 bodyweight squats. Sounds small. It's not. Your brain will remember you tried.

5. Plank (or wall push-up)

Hold a plank for 20 seconds. Or do wall push-ups. Strength counts. Even small.

6. Stretch (10 minutes)

YouTube has thousands of options. Pick one. Gentle counts. You're still moving.

7. Take the stairs

Going somewhere? Take the stairs instead. That's your workout today.

8. Do arm circles + leg lifts

Stand up. 20 arm circles each way. 10 leg lifts on each side. 2 minutes. Done.

9. Follow a 5-minute YouTube workout

Search "5-minute workout" and pick any video. Commit to just 5 minutes. You can do 5.

10. Just show up (literally stand there)

If nothing else: put on workout clothes, stand in your workout space, and breathe. You showed up. That's the first step. Tomorrow will be easier.

If you've checked off at least one of these, today counts as a win. Move on. Rest is a full day of activity.
